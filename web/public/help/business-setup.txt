Your business
1. In guided setup, enter your business name.
2. Choose Shop or retail, Travel or bookings, NGO/church/foundation, School/training centre, or Event planning. Sedifex seeds recommended pages when navigation has not already been configured.
3. Expand Contact and location if you want to add a business phone, country, town or address. These details can be added later.
4. Choose Save and continue. Existing navigation and dashboard customizations are preserved.

First task and ready screen
Choose the recommended first task or Explore the dashboard. Continue to the ready screen, then open that task. Travel opens a Service draft; School opens a Course draft. Review and save the item yourself. Setup does not publish your business publicly.
Finish later saves your current draft and opens the dashboard. Return using Continue guided setup in the business setup checklist. Progress is saved when you continue or finish later, not on every keystroke.

Complete the checklist when useful
Optional steps may be skipped. A skipped step is not counted as completed. Refresh progress after saving changes. Staff use their assigned workspace pages; owners manage business setup.
1. Business profile: save your business name and contact information in Account. A logo is optional.
2. Products or services: create your first item.
3. Payout account: open Payout account and complete the available Paystack setup before relying on online payment routing.
4. Public page: save and enable your page in Public page settings, then preview its customer view.
