Set up your business workspace

Your business

  1. In guided setup, enter your business name.
  2. Choose Shop or retail, Travel or bookings, NGO/church/foundation, School/training centre, or Event planning. Sedifex seeds recommended pages when navigation has not already been configured.
  3. Expand Contact and location if you want to add a business phone, country, town or address. These details can be added later.
  4. Choose Save and continue. Existing navigation and dashboard customizations are preserved.

First task and ready screen

Choose the recommended first task or Explore the dashboard. Continue to the ready screen, then open that task. Travel opens a Service draft; School opens a Course draft. Review and save the item yourself. Setup does not publish your business publicly.

Finish later saves your draft and opens the dashboard. Your initial onboarding ends here, and no profile reminder appears on Dashboard or Account. You can update your business profile anytime in Account.

Profile details after signup

The dashboard does not show an additional profile setup checklist. Products, services, payouts and website integration are separate tools you can use whenever needed.

  1. Add your business name when signing up or during the first onboarding step.
  2. You can add optional contact and location information then, or edit it later in Account → Business details.
